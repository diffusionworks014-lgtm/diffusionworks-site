precipitation

Recycling, regulation and next-generation cells · noun

Hydrometallurgical step in which dissolved metal ions in a leach or purified solution are converted into an insoluble solid by adding a reagent or adjusting pH, used to remove impurities or to recover products such as lithium carbonate or mixed hydroxide.
